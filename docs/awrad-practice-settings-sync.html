Shared practice rules, local device context

Account policy becomes canonical after login. Each device retains its own location and timezone; prayer times and the effective practice day are derived from that local context.

01 / Architecture

Shared policy in the middle; each device resolves its own day

The account policy syncs to every device. Each device registers its own location and timezone and resolves its own effective practice day; two devices may legitimately be on different practice dates at the same instant.

02 / First-login sequence

Local settings are a draft until the account is bound

Only AccountPracticePolicy participates in the conflict. The current device’s location and timezone are retained and registered separately; counts continue through the ledger and merge independently.

03 / State machine

An account policy can become canonical, conflict, or stop

This state machine covers the shared policy only. Location and timezone live in each device context and never enter the policy conflict. A second account never silently inherits the first account’s local store.

Policy has one owner

  • Before login: local policy is a draft.
  • After login: account policy is canonical.
  • Location and timezone stay per device.

Counts keep provenance

  • Count credits merge through the existing ledger.
  • Historical dates are never re-bucketed.
  • New counts carry policy + device context revisions.

Context stays private

  • Each device keeps its own location.
  • Web may use manual or browser location.
  • Account switching never silently rebinds data.
