Recommended definitions
  1. Total count
    Sum of canonical daily totals across all goals and slots for this dhikr.
  2. Active-day average
    Total divided by days whose combined dhikr count is greater than zero.
  3. Presence
    Active days divided by days in the selected calendar window. Descriptive, not adherence.
  4. Adherence
    Completed scheduled opportunities divided by due opportunities; kept separate from presence.
  5. Affinity evidence
    A transparent interpretation of recurrence, retention, adherence, and recency—not spiritual merit.
Known data limit
The current daily buckets do not retain session start time or every tap. “Favorite time,” session duration, and session count would be invented unless a new event/session fact is introduced.

Proposed read model

Keep counts as the source of truth

Build a logical Dhikr Day by joining a count entry to its goal, taking the goal's dhikr, then summing every positive count for the same user, dhikr, and effective date.

Dhikr Day can be a Room query / server SQL view first. Do not persist totals, averages, streaks, or affinity scores until query cost proves that a cached projection is needed.

Metric contract

Recorded totalΣ dhikr_day.total over the selected window.
Active dayAn effective date where dhikr_day.total > 0.
Active-day averagerecorded_total ÷ active_days. Null when there are no active days.
Calendar-day averagerecorded_total ÷ window_days. Useful, but never label it simply “daily average.”
Current streakConsecutive active effective days ending today, or yesterday while today remains open.
Presenceactive_days ÷ window_days. It does not know whether a day was scheduled.
Adherencecompleted_due_opportunities ÷ due_opportunities from goal schedules.
Affinity evidenceExplainable signals. Repetition volume is displayed but excluded from scoring.

Deletion and lifetime semantics

The current mobile model removes count history when a goal (or custom dhikr) is physically deleted. Therefore an aggregation over current rows is a recorded total, not a guaranteed lifetime total. If lifetime history must survive goal deletion, preserve a tombstoned goal/count relationship or introduce a durable dhikr-keyed history fact before using the word “lifetime.”

Affinity guardrails

  • Use the score internally or as an opt-in experiment; lead with plain-language evidence in the product.
  • Do not compare raw repetition volume across dhikrs with different prescribed targets.
  • Show a confidence label based on the amount of observed history.
  • Call the result a practice pattern, not a measure of devotion, reward, or spiritual quality.
