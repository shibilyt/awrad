# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muslims building or maintaining a daily dhikr habit, including casual practitioners, users with ambitious counting goals, people following structured wird programs, and users who connect practice to prayer times. They use Awrad in short daily sessions and longer reflective reading or counting sessions, across English, Arabic, and Malayalam.

## Product Purpose

Awrad helps users sustain remembrance of Allah through structured goals, manual and audio-assisted counting, prayer-time awareness, progress tracking, and structured wird reading. Success means the app supports a consistent spiritual practice without adding friction or requiring connectivity for core practice.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, spiritual. The experience should feel serene, focused, warm, and unobtrusive so the interface recedes behind the devotional task.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense or busy dashboards, hard-edged or stylistically mixed controls, heavy drop shadows, large decorative accent surfaces, and visual treatments that compete with Arabic text or the practice itself. Do not reduce the product to a generic counter or productivity dashboard.

## Design Principles

1. Practice comes first: keep counting and reading focused, legible, and interruption-free.
2. Calm clarity: use familiar mobile patterns and a restrained hierarchy that supports reflection.
3. Respect spiritual context: preserve authentic content, prayer awareness, Islamic-day behavior, and meaningful pacing.
4. Work locally: core dhikr, goals, counting, and wird flows remain useful offline.
5. Design for the full community: treat dark mode, Arabic RTL, multilingual content, and a wide range of practice intensity as first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and icons, maintain at least 48dp interactive targets, provide localized content descriptions, support Arabic RTL layout and generous Arabic line height, and never communicate meaning through color alone.
